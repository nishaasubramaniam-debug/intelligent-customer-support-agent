FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How can I contact support?

A: Customers can contact support through the online support portal.

Q: What are the support hours?

A: Customer support is available Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Q: How long do returns take?

A: Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery. Refunds are generally processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product has been received and inspected.

Q: How can I check my order?

A: Customers should provide their order ID to check order status.

Q: How can I change my shipping address or profile details?

A: You can change your shipping address by navigating to Account Settings > Addresses in your profile dashboard. If you have an active order that hasn't shipped yet, provide your order ID or contact customer support immediately to request an address update before dispatch.

Q: Can I cancel my order after it has shipped?

A: Orders that have already been shipped may not be cancelled.

Q: What information should I never share with support?

A: Customers should never share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or complete payment card details.
