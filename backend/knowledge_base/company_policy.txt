COMPANY CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

Our customer support team is available Monday to Saturday
from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Customers can contact support through the online support
portal.

Customers should provide their order ID when asking about
orders.

Support requests involving payment disputes may require
additional verification.

Customers should never share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or
complete payment card details with customer support.

For security-related issues, the support team may request
additional verification before providing account information.
