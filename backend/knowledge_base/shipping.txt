SHIPPING & ADDRESS CHANGE POLICY

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 7 business days.

CHANGING SHIPPING ADDRESS:
- Before Shipping (Status: Processing): Yes, customers can change their delivery address by contacting support or updating order details before the package is shipped.
- After Shipping (Status: Shipped / Out for Delivery): Address changes CANNOT be guaranteed or made once the order has already been dispatched. In such cases, customers must contact human support for courier re-routing assistance.

Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled.

Delivery times may vary depending on location and external delivery conditions.

If an order has not arrived after the expected delivery period, the customer should contact customer support with their Order ID.
