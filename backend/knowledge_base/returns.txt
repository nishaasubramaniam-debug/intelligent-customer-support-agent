RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and in their original packaging.

Some products may not be eligible for return.

Refunds are processed after the returned product has been
received and inspected.

Refund processing usually takes 5 to 7 business days.

If a product is damaged or defective, the customer should
contact customer support as soon as possible.

The customer should provide the order ID when requesting a
return or refund.
